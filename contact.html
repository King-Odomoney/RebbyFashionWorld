Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an appointment to visit?

Walk-ins are welcome during business hours! However, we recommend booking an appointment for personalized service and to ensure we give you our full attention.

How long does custom tailoring take?

Typically 1-2 weeks depending on the complexity of the design. For urgent requests, please let us know and we'll do our best to accommodate your timeline.

Do you offer alterations for clothes not made by you?

Yes! We provide professional alteration services for garments from any source. Bring in your outfit and we'll help you achieve the perfect fit.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, bank transfers, and mobile money payments. A deposit is typically required to begin custom work.

Can I bring my own fabric?

Absolutely! You're welcome to bring your own fabric. We can also help you source high-quality fabrics if needed.

Do you handle bulk orders?

Yes! We specialize in bulk orders for corporate uniforms, wedding parties, and group events. Contact us for a custom quote.
